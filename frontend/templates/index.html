🚨 Historical Case Studies

Major Cyber Incidents in History

Real-world lessons from catastrophic cyber breaches and social engineering campaigns.

Ransomware
WannaCry (2017)

Exploited unpatched Windows SMB vulnerability (EternalBlue). Infected 200,000+ computers across 150 nations within hours, crippling NHS hospitals in the UK.

Lesson: Keep systems patched immediately.
Social Engineering
Twitter VIP Hijack (2020)

Teens phoned Twitter employees pretending to be internal IT helpdesk, obtaining admin access to hijack accounts of Elon Musk, Barack Obama, and Apple for Bitcoin fraud.

Lesson: Always verify out-of-band identity.
Credential Reuse
Colonial Pipeline (2021)

A single compromised legacy VPN password without Two-Factor Authentication forced the shutdown of the largest US fuel pipeline, causing nationwide panic and shortages.

Lesson: Mandate Multi-Factor Auth (MFA).
Zero-Click Spyware
Pegasus Spyware

Military-grade surveillance spyware developed by NSO Group capable of zero-click infections on iOS/Android via silent WhatsApp packets to monitor microphone, GPS, and chats.

Lesson: Enable Lockdown Mode & reboot phones.
⚖️ Legal Framework & Incident Reporting

Victim of a Cyber Attack or Online Fraud?

Under the Information Technology (IT) Act, 2000, cyber offences carry stringent penalties and prison sentences. In case of financial fraud or harassment, report within the "Golden Hour" for immediate account freezing.

📞 National Helpline

🌐 Official Portal

File formal online FIR complaints
📜 Key Sections of IT Act, 2000
  • Sec 43: Penalty for damage to computer systems, data theft, and unauthorized access (Up to ₹1 Crore).
  • Sec 66C: Identity theft, stealing digital signatures or passwords (Imprisonment up to 3 years).
  • Sec 66D: Cheating by personation using computer resource / Phishing (Imprisonment up to 3 years).
  • Sec 66E: Violation of bodily privacy and voyeurism via digital devices.
